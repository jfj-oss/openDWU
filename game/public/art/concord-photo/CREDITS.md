# Concord photo art: sources

The PNGs in this folder are derived art. `scripts/concord-photo-art.py` builds them from the specimen photos below by
cutting each one out, straightening it (and for some, mirroring it from its better-preserved half), recolouring it,
relighting it and adding procedural cybernetics. The JSON files beside the PNGs hold the light and thruster positions
the build step drew, and the limb pieces with their attachment points. The source photos themselves are not in the repo. All of them are in the public domain, and the
licence of each was checked on its Wikimedia Commons file page (the licence template is noted in brackets).

`<kind>-limbs.png` holds each insect's legs and antennae, cut from `<kind>.png` (same pixels), for the close-zoom
animation. The egg sac's membrane, egg layout, cradle ring and docking arms, and every cybernetic part (with its rust and
wear), are procedural; the eggs carry the photo's own egg texture. The Exchange space port has its own `port.png` (same source, arms spread evenly). No original game art was used.
