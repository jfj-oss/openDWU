# Sim soak 2026-09-26: 5 seeds × 2 game years

Read-mostly health pass on branch `wip/soak` (base `8f9cd19`). **Nothing in `src/` was changed.** The only code change is
to the headless runner `scripts/sim-run.mjs`, which gained observers. None of them touches game state (see Harness).

## Setup

- Seeds 1, 2, 3, 7, 11. 700 stars, 8×8 sectors, 10 empires (player + 9 AI), default wizard settings (age 1, tech 0.5,
  pirates 1). 1200 game-s each = 730 game days (`RealSecondsInGalacticYear` 600).
- Runs were one at a time on a shared box (loadavg 20–35):
  ```
  node --expose-gc scripts/sim-run.mjs --seed N --stars 700 --empires 10 --sectors 8 --seconds 1200 \
       --stats-days 30 --stats-json sN.json --json runN.json
  ```
- Every 30 game days `--stats-days 30` prints:
  - per empire: colonies, population, money, cashflow (`CalculateAnnualCashflow`), ships by sub role (state and private),
    ships under construction, idle ships (no mission) and ships below 10% fuel, each with a "≥ 90 days" count, projects
    researched, characters, relation types;
  - galaxy totals: wars and treaties (pairs), ships destroyed (empire/pirate), pirate ships and their missions, messages
    by `EmpireMessageType`, NaN scan (money, cashflow, populations, ship positions and fuel), console errors, exceptions,
    TODO(port) hits, ms/frame and ms per frame-driver pass;
  - lists: each built colony ship (mission, target, distance, speed, fuel, first commands), ships idle or low on fuel
    for ≥ 90 days, and ships waiting in HyperTo.
- `--watch-money "Empire:threshold"` logs every change to that empire's `stateMoney` at or above the threshold, with the
  sim call stack, plus the empire's cashflow terms at each snapshot.

## Anomalies

Nothing here was a *small, clear* deviation from the C#, so all 8 are written up and none were fixed. For each I list
the evidence, the suspected code and the C# comparison.

### A1. Expansion is still very slow: first colony ships complete at day 438–690, and 0–5 of 10 empires have a second colony by day 730 — **wrong data (suspected)**
- **Evidence:** see the table. Seed 3 has **no** new colony in 2 years. Colony ships spend ~400–500 days in the capital's single colony
  yard. With speed 600 (all capitals reach 600 by day 60), `ConstructionQueue.cs 313-318` gives
  `600/1000 / ColonyShipBuildFactor 10 × ColonyShipBuildSpeedRate` ≈ 0.06 components/s, which is ~36 components per year
  for a 39–44-component ship. The per-yard formula and the "colony ships only in colony yards" rule were already checked
  in fix6 (`4dd49eb`, `empireConstruction.ts` ~1745).
- **Still open:** DW:U AI empires on "Starting" expansion normally found colonies well inside year 1.
  - For AI empires `ColonyShipBuildSpeedRate` = `1/DifficultyLevel` (`BaconGalaxy.cs 151`, `pirates.ts:167`). Confirm that
    DifficultyLevel is ≤ 1 for AI at default difficulty.
  - Check whether an AI in the C# also buys colony ships through other paths (e.g. `Empire.6.cs` colony-ship purchase at a
    space port when `flag3` is false). It never happened in 5 seeds × 2 years.
- **Repro:** any seed. `sim-run … --stats-days 30`, then read `colship` lines and the `colony ships:` summary.
- **Files:** `src/sim/construction/empireConstruction.ts` `directConstruction` (1742+),
  `src/sim/construction/constructionQueue.ts:337-404`, `pirates.ts:160-170`.

### A2. No war is declared in any seed within 2 years — **suspected**
- **Evidence:** `wars 0` in all 125 snapshots. Relations reach FTA, TradeSanctions (seeds 1, 3, 7, 11) and
  `DiplomaticRelationChange` "trade terminated". `RequestJointWar` and `RequestStopWar` never appear.
- **Expected:** with 2–6 contacts per empire and sanctions already in place, the C# AI sometimes declares war in year 2.
  It is not guaranteed: `CheckCanConductNewWar` and `CheckReadyForWar` need military strength and a target.
- **Next step:** instrument `diplomacyTick.ts:1678-1700` (the top-strategy pick → `checkCanConductNewWar` →

### A8. Per-tick cost grows 1.3–1.6× over 2 years, mostly from HyperTo → FastFindNearestSystem — **performance, faithful**
- **CPU ms/frame per 200 game-s** (cpu time, less sensitive to machine load than wall time):

  | seed | 0–200 | 200–400 | 400–600 | 600–800 | 800–1000 | 1000–1200 | builtObjects |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | 1.99 | 2.21 | 1.97 | 1.73 | 2.41 | 2.14 | 1388 → 1713 |
  | 2 | 1.65 | 1.91 | 3.08 | 3.04 | 3.12 | 3.02 | 1310 → 1585 |
  | 3 | 2.04 | 2.18 | 2.40 | 2.06 | 2.17 | 2.61 | 1413 → 1720 |
  | 7 | 2.01 | 2.08 | 2.55 | 2.62 | 2.99 | 2.88 | 1371 → 1689 |
  | 11 | 1.96 | 1.96 | 2.39 | 2.45 | 2.65 | 2.71 | 1229 → 1492 |

  Heap after GC ends at 128–136 MB in every run (start 104–111 MB), so no leak.
- **Which subsystem:** the frame-driver timer puts 85–90% of the time in the `builtObjects` pass in every snapshot, and
  that pass drives the growth. The `habitats` pass is ~0.2–0.4 ms/frame and everything else is < 0.1.
- **Profile of seed 2, year 1** (`--profile`, 62 s sampled):
  - `builtObjectDoTasks > executeCommands` 26.6 s, of which `cmdHyperTo` 14.8 s.
  - Inside `cmdHyperTo`: `checkFightersOnboardAndRetrieve` → `isOutsideStarGravityWell` → `fastFindNearestSystem` →
    `ringSearch` **10.0 s (16% of the run)**.
  - Next: `handleWeaponsFiringBuiltObject` 3.4 s, `rechargeShields` 1.7 s.
- **Why:** `cmdHyperTo` (`missions/cmdMovement.ts:253`) calls `checkFightersOnboardAndRetrieve` on every frame of a jump.
  In hyperspace `nearestSystemStar` is null, so `isOutsideStarGravityWell` (`movement.ts:887`) runs a full
  `fastFindNearestSystem` ring search on every frame. This happens even though BaconSettings has
  `useStarGravityWells=false`, because the flag is checked after the search. The C# does the same
  (`BaconBuiltObject.cs 2493-2511`; `Galaxy.6.cs 3648 FastFindNearestSystem`). The cost scales with the number of ships in
  hyperspace, which rises with pirate fleets (~50 → ~200) and freighters.
- **Optimisation option (not done, behaviour-neutral):** memoise `fastFindNearestSystem` per galaxy index cell, or skip
  the search while the ship is in hyperspace. The search has no Rnd draws. The side effect
  `ship.nearestSystemStar = habitat` only fires within `MaxSolarSystemSize` of a star, which a ship in hyperspace rarely
  is, so it would still need the same result.

## Harness notes

- The stats observers were checked against a plain run: seed 1, 60 s, same chunk plan, digest `a1ad986c7e993230` both ways.
- `calculateAnnualCashflow` → `ThisYearsSpacePortIncome` (`Empire.cs 2282`) resets per-base yearly income when read in
  non-averaged mode, so the observer reads it with `useAveragedVariableIncome` temporarily true.
- `runGameSeconds` ends on a frame boundary, so **different** chunk plans can give different frame counts (3601 vs 3602
  frames for 60 s) and different digests. Compare digests only with identical `--chunk` / `--probe-days` / `--stats-days`.
- Headless, the player's `CheckAgeVariableIncome` never runs (UI-only, `Main.Part11.cs 841`). The player's space-port
  income therefore stays in non-averaged mode, as it would in the C# with the money panel never refreshed.
